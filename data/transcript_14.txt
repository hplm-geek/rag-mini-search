Transcript 14 — Intro Call
Prospect: Christine H.
Industry: Executive Coaching
Date: 2024-11-20

Sean: Christine, what's the focus for today?

Christine: I coach senior executives — C-suite, VPs, people navigating big transitions. I've been doing this for nine years, so I know the coaching side cold. But I've recently been thinking about the business more seriously and I keep hitting the same wall.

Sean: What's the wall?

Christine: I can't productively delegate anything because nothing is documented. I've been thinking about bringing on an associate coach to expand capacity. But every time I get close, I realize I'd have to teach them everything from scratch because none of my frameworks, intake process, or client management approach is written down anywhere.

Sean: So growth is blocked by undocumented process.

Christine: Exactly. And the irony is I help executives build organizational capacity for a living. My own business doesn't have any of that.

Sean: What does your tech stack look like?

Christine: Very light. A scheduling tool, a video platform, and a shared Google Drive folder. That's about it. I've intentionally kept things simple but I think simple has crossed into under-built.

Sean: There's a difference between simple and designed-to-be-simple. What you're describing sounds like it defaulted to simple — meaning it happened by omission rather than intention.

Christine: That's a sharp distinction. Yes. I didn't design for simplicity. I just never built anything.

Sean: The good news is starting light actually makes this easier. There's less to untangle. We'd start with documenting what already works in your head and turning it into something reproducible.

Christine: That would be the foundation I need. I've been stuck at this ceiling for two years.
