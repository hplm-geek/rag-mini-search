Transcript 15 — Intro Call
Prospect: Brandon J.
Industry: Real Estate Investment Coaching
Date: 2024-08-14

Sean: Brandon, what's the main challenge?

Brandon: I coach new real estate investors — people who want to get into buy-and-hold or short-term rentals but don't know where to start. Good demand for what I do. But I've grown too fast and I'm disorganized in a way that's starting to affect my clients.

Sean: Tell me more about the client experience side.

Brandon: I have a 12-week program. But the structure lives in my head. Some clients are at week 4, some at week 7, some have skipped modules because I let them jump around. I don't have a dashboard or even a simple tracker that shows me where everyone is.

Sean: What happens when a client comes to a call?

Brandon: I check my notes from the last call, which I write in a Google Doc that's just a long running document with dates. It works but it's clunky. And if I'm honest, I sometimes have to read back further than I should to remember what we were working on.

Sean: What about the program itself — is there content, worksheets, templates?

Brandon: Yes — I've built a good amount of content. It's spread across a Google Drive folder, Teachable, and some stuff is still just in my email because I created it for a specific client and never formalized it.

Sean: So IP is being created but not captured in any organized way.

Brandon: Right. And I keep building new stuff instead of organizing what I have. Last week I created a new deal analysis template and then realized I probably made one six months ago and can't find it.

Sean: That's a very common pattern. The solution isn't more creation — it's an intake and retrieval system for what already exists. Once that's in place, you start leveraging what you've built instead of recreating it.

Brandon: I think I've been avoiding this because I don't know where to start. It feels overwhelming.

Sean: We always start with one workflow, not the whole system. Usually the one that's costing you the most time or causing the most client-facing friction. For you that's probably the program tracker.
