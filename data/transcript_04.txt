Transcript 04 — Intro Call
Prospect: James P.
Industry: Executive Search / Recruiting
Date: 2024-08-22

Sean: James, what's going on in your world right now?

James: I run a boutique exec search firm — just me and one part-time researcher. We do well but I've hit a ceiling. I can't take on more searches without dropping quality, and I can't grow without building something more systematic.

Sean: Where does it break down currently?

James: Candidate tracking is a mess. I use LinkedIn Recruiter, a spreadsheet, and my own memory. My researcher and I are constantly out of sync. Last month I accidentally reached out to a candidate I'd already declined six months ago. That's the kind of thing that shouldn't happen.

Sean: What about your client communication side?

James: Even worse. Status updates are ad hoc. I send them when I remember. A client asked me for a search summary last week and I had to reconstruct it from my inbox.

Sean: Have you looked at ATS platforms?

James: I trialed two. They're built for agencies with 15 recruiters. Way too heavy. I don't need enterprise software — I need something clean that the two of us can actually use and maintain.

Sean: That's exactly the pattern. Off-the-shelf tools are often built for scale you don't have yet. What you need is a lightweight system designed around your actual search workflow — not a generic recruiting process.

James: That's a relief to hear. I was starting to think I'd have to just hire more people to manage the chaos.

Sean: You might need people eventually. But right now you need the system first — or whoever you hire just inherits the chaos.

James: That's a really good point. I hadn't thought of it that way.
