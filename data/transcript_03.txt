Transcript 03 — Intro Call
Prospect: Diane R.
Industry: Online Course Creator / Health & Wellness
Date: 2024-11-01

Sean: Hi Diane, great to meet you. What prompted you to reach out now?

Diane: Honestly, a LinkedIn post you wrote about execution friction. It stopped me mid-scroll because I thought you were describing my business.

Sean: What resonated?

Diane: The part about the system never being designed — it just accumulated. That's mine. I have a course on burnout recovery that's doing really well. I launched a second one in September and now I feel like I'm in the exact burnout spiral I teach people to get out of. The irony is not lost on me.

Sean: Walk me through what's breaking down.

Diane: My content calendar exists in my head. My student onboarding is three different emails I copy-paste manually. My revenue tracking is a Google Sheet I update when I remember. I had 47 unread support messages last week and I'm the only one answering them. I don't have a team, I have tools that don't connect and tasks that don't have owners.

Sean: What have you tried to fix it?

Diane: I bought a Notion template for course creators. I set it up over a weekend. Used it for about 10 days, then stopped because it didn't match how I actually work. I think I need someone to build it with me, not just hand me a template.

Sean: That's the difference between a system designed for an ideal day versus one built around your actual workflow. Most templates fail for exactly that reason.

Diane: Yes. And I don't have time for another failed system. I have a third course in draft and I'm scared to launch it because I can't even manage what I have.

Sean: What you're describing is very solvable. But we'd start with one thing — probably the student onboarding — and make just that repeatable. One win, documented, before anything else.

Diane: That would genuinely change my week. The onboarding alone would save me hours.
