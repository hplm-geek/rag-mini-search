Transcript 01 — Intro Call
Prospect: Laura M.
Industry: Business Coaching
Date: 2024-10-04

Sean: Thanks for making time, Laura. Tell me a little about what's going on in your business right now.

Laura: Sure. So I've been coaching for about four years, mostly leadership development for mid-level managers transitioning into senior roles. Business is actually pretty good — I'm almost fully booked. But here's the thing, I'm exhausted and I can't figure out why because the numbers look fine.

Sean: What does a typical week look like operationally?

Laura: Chaotic, honestly. I'm the one handling all the scheduling, intake forms, follow-up emails, invoicing, even my own social content. I tried Notion for a while, then switched to Airtable, and now I have stuff in both and neither one is current. Every Monday morning I spend like 90 minutes just figuring out what I'm supposed to be doing that week.

Sean: So the business is running, but it's running through you — not through a system.

Laura: That's exactly it. When I left corporate I thought I'd finally have control. Instead I'm just doing all the jobs I used to delegate. I feel like I'm holding everything in my head and one bad week and something slips.

Sean: What have you tried so far besides the tools?

Laura: I hired a VA for three months. It helped a little but I realized I didn't have anything documented so I was basically training her in real time on stuff I'd never actually written down. She left and I was back to square one.

Sean: What would a win look like for you in the next 30 days?

Laura: Honestly? A Monday morning that doesn't feel like triage. Just knowing the week is set up and I don't have to reconstruct it from scratch every time.

Sean: That's a systems problem, not a time problem. The work exists — it just lives in your head instead of somewhere that runs without you holding it. That's what we fix.

Laura: That's — yeah. That's exactly the problem. Nobody's ever said it that plainly.
